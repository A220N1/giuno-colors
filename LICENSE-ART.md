The drawings in `drawings/albums/` are traced from coloring albums illustrated by Jean Matet and published from 1922 to 1925, digitized by the Bibliothèque nationale de France in Gallica and shared on Wikimedia Commons. Each album page to color has a printed colored model next to it, which gives the zones their colors. The albums are in the public domain in the United States, where they were published before 1931. The BnF catalog names the illustrator "Matet, Jean" without dates. He is very probably Jean François Matet (1870-1936), a painter and illustrator of children's books in the BnF catalog, whose works entered the public domain in France in 2007, but the BnF does not link the two records.
